Team sites

One passcode-gated site per team. Changing a passcode REPUBLISHES that team's site — live on Pages in ~a minute, but browsers cache the page for up to 10 minutes, so an already-open site needs a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) before the new code opens it. Passcodes live in the bot's .env; hand them to teams privately.
