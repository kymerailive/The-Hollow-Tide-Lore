📗 Drafted location sheets

A drafted sheet contributes only its LOCATION and ABOUT lines, so it grounds nothing an NPC says there. Worst first: places with players standing on them, then the longest unreviewed writing. Clearing a sheet is the review — open it in Locations, untick Draft, and save.
